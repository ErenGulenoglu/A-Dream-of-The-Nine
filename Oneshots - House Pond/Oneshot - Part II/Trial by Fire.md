## Summary
This small adventure explores the aftermath of the succession crisis in [[House Pond]], in the late summer of **857 AA**, after [[Sir Gregory Pond]] won the succession trial against the cadet branch of his house, _Green Pond_.

The time skip begins when Sir Gregory arrives at [[Basilica Arbitria]] in [[Ewyngard]], the capital of [[The Senate and People of Utland|SPQU]], with [[Sir Alexander Brokentower, the Shieldbreaker]] to meet with His Holiness [[Vicar Lucius Berklen]], as a consequence of magic being used in the trial by combat against [[Sir Robyn Grape]]. His Holiness asks Sir Gregory to display his powers in his private study. Understanding the nature of Sir Gregory's abilities, His Holiness asks Gregory to keep them secret, and requires him to swear that he shall never use them for selfish purposes or against the innocent souls of the realm, lest the curse of the gods be upon him. After Gregory swears the oath, he is free to leave, and the time skip ends.

At the start of the adventure, Sir Gregory travels along the main road leading into his homeland, [[Ample Grove]]. There he comes across a small group of bandits who attempt to rob him before letting him go. These individuals are [[Kaltik]] refugees who were driven from their homes by orc tribes moving east from [[Giant Foothills]] after the collapse of the Kingdom of Utland. Over time, these refugees turned to banditry to earn a living, as they were driven away wherever they went within the SPQU. Eventually, they migrated to Ample Grove after [[Diethard Farkvel|Baron Diethard Farkvel]] allowed them to travel east, provided they did not remain within his lands. Their presence was soon discovered by [[Zahar Pond]], Gregory's eldest cousin, who seeks to rule House Pond and its lands. Zahar hired the Kaltik refugees to wage irregular warfare in [[Ample Grove]] against Sir Gregory, with the goal of killing him if possible, or otherwise weakening his forces until he is forced to surrender or concede land, in return promising a land for Kaltiks to settle.

The Kaltik refugees are led by twin brothers [[Kroman]] and [[Karon]], who found opportunity in banditry in exile. They gathered the men of their tribe around themselves, promising to provide for their families with gold, and if they managed to kill the lord of [[Knight's Rest]], a place where they could finally live in peace without being driven out by others, as they themselves once had been. Kroman and Karon believe that the SPQU does not accept its own people, with nobles driving away and pushing out those who only want to earn an honest living. They believe ones that driven them away from honest life deserve to experience what they had.

The tribe has established its camp within the forest, protected by traps and guarded at all times. They conduct hit-and-run raids on villages, killing any guards they encounter on sight and anyone who attempts to resist. If the guards withdraw, they assume the role of the local militia, looting harvests, game, and crops, while sharing the spoils only with families whose at least one member joins their ranks. Because of this, many villagers have fled to [[Appleton]], seeking safety closer to Knight's Rest.

However, the tribe itself is divided. One half remains loyal to Kroman and supports banditry, believing that, after being rejected everywhere they went in Utland, the only way to secure a future is to carve one out by force. The other half wishes to leave violence behind and establish a new home by reaching an agreement with the local authorities instead of forcing the people into submission.

Gregory must decide how to deal with the situation, as his time, resources, and household guards continue to dwindle. At [[Knight's Rest]], his eldest brother **Garibald**, who survived the dragon fire, watches him closely and serves as an old guardian of the house, judging whether Gregory is worthy to lead House Pond through a time of crisis.

The adventure concludes with one of several possible outcomes: Gregory assaults the refugees' camp under favourable or unfavourable conditions, or he reaches an agreement that allows the refugees to settle, on either favourable or unfavourable terms.
## Cato
- Cato is a false name adopted by [[Garibald Pond]], the eldest son of [[Sir Maryn Pond]].
- After hearing of his father's death, Garibald travelled to the north of the realm before eventually returning home. Shortly after his return, the Kaltik bandits arrived.
- Garibald wants to clear his homeland of the bandits before leaving again.
- He wants to see what kind of man his younger brother, Gregory, has become, and whether he can lead House Pond into the future.
## Scene 0: Time Skip
- After Gregory won his trial against his cousin Zahar, he became Lord Pond, and inherited all lands and belongings of House Pond.
- As his use of magic was discovered by Sir Alexander Brokentower, he did not expose him directly as he perceived Sir Gregory was an honorable man in his core, keeping his knighthood oaths he took beside his fathers' deathbed at all cost, also with the fact that Gregory was the last son of his close friend, Sir Maryn Pond. Sir Alexander proposed Gregory to take him to the capital, [[Ewyngard]] and leave his fate to the hands of his holiness [[Vicar Lucius Berklen]] against others who might have discovered his spellcaster nature. And Gregory accepted this offer, for he wanted to gain protection of [[The Church of The Eight]] against accuses of witchery.
- After two months of travel to the capital, the two knights have reached to [[Basilica Arbitria]], the central administrative cell of the Church of the Eight, also known as the city within city. Here, Sir Alexander arranged a private meeting with his holiness for Gregory.
- In this meeting the spellcasting nature of Sir Gregory was revealed to his holiness, Sir Alexander reporting that he perceived no evil acts or intention of Gregory, and vouching that Gregory's family was royal to Utland. Gregory is asked to put his right hand in holy water, drink holy water, and pray to gods to prove that his spellcasting nature contains no evil. Finally, he is asked to swear that he shall never use them for selfish purposes or against the innocent souls of the realm, lest the curse of the gods be upon him. Once he mad the oath, he was free to go. His holiness adds that, when the time comes, he might be asked to put his abilities to good use for the realm of the awakened.
- Sir Alexander gifted Gregory a white painted **+1 Shield** with steel rim, core made of oak, with a red symbol of the Eight on in. And, Gregory sets his way to his home, to Ample Grove.
- Would Gregory travel back to his home with bodyguards, or alone? Would he keep a low profile on the road, or wear colors of his house or an armor?

## Scene 2: Knight's Rest

```
As you draw closer to Blue Pond, a large fortified manor with blue roof tiles and gray stone walls reveals itself among the trees on the western shore of the lake. High outer walls extend toward the forest, denying any easy approach or immediate chance of capture. The lakeside foundations of the manor are built directly into Blue Pond itself. The doors facing the lake are thick, iron-bound, and defensible against sieges, forcing anyone wishing to reach the lakeside platform to swim.

The walls of the manor have more arrow slits than windows, and guards can be seen stationed from the highest level of the manor down to the outer walls. A flag with the green fish on blue field of House Pond waves from the highest tip of the manor. The militaristic nature of House Pond shaped this manor into more of a castle than a residence, a fortress built in what seemed like a paradise beyond the reach of enemies, yet always prepared for the day they might come. You reach Knight's Rest, your home.
```
- The guardsmen will be cautious about anyone who gets close to the manor. But currently, the manor is not surrounded, except for one watcher, as the Kaltik bandits are in Appleton with their full force of 60 men, forcibly recruiting and looting from the villagers.
- When Gregory enters the manor, he is greeted by Captain Titus and given a brief overview of the ongoing crisis.
    - 80 guards remain serving House Pond. 20 have been killed in the past month. The bandits are hiding inside the forest and setting traps.
    - [[Iris Lavoine]] left the defense and recapturing of the villages to Captain of the household guard Titus.
    - A group of bandits came to the center of Partia a month ago, killing guards and looting villages unexpectedly. Captain Titus does not know how this group managed to pass through other lords' lands to arrive in Ample Grove, and specifically attack House Pond rather than [[House Moon]].
- Captain Titus explains his plan in detail in the command room. The command room is situated in one of the rooms dug beneath the ground. It has a large wooden table in its middle, with oil lamps on the table lighting the room. Bookshelves cover the walls, with a map of Ample Grove and stones representing the forces stationed on the map, alongside many books opened on the table.
    - Inside the command room, there are 3 advisors: Captain Titus, Garibald Pond in disguise as Cato, and Iris Lavoine.
    - Iris Lavoine does not trust Cato and is not sure if he is truly loyal. He arrived a week before the bandits entered the grove, but the royal signature of House Pond he provided is genuine. She thinks he might be a spy. Iris at first promised safe passage for the refugees, but they did not leave and instead killed the guards, gaining the upper hand before betraying them.
        - Iris thinks that these bandits were sent here on purpose. At first, she thought they were refugees coming from the west, and when she had an audience with one of them, the man told her they came from the west. But now she realizes that only with Baron Farkvel's permission could they have come from western Partia.
        - Iris has asked [[House Moon]] on the southern side of Ampol for help, but they answered that they would not send any aid.
    - Captain Titus thinks that burning the grove will cripple House Pond in the long term, and is in conflict with Cato. He thinks they need to clear out the grove through diplomacy and with the help of the Kaltik refugees, who can provide information from inside.
        - He arranged for an insider who can share the positions of traps and guards. The insider's name is Faron, a young Kaltik who fell in love with one of the girls in Appleton. _This option leads to the optional scene._
    - Cato thinks the only way to drive the bandits out of the forest is to burn it. He thinks the bandits specifically attacking northern Ample Grove is not a coincidence, and they will not leave as long as they do not achieve what they seek in the forest. They prefer asymmetrical warfare methods and will not engage in a fight where they do not have the numbers. It is impossible to gain the upper hand in the forest with the traps and the freedom to maneuver they have. So, outflanking all of them and burning the forest from the north to the Blue Lake creeks, an area of 25 km², is the best way in his view to push these bandits against the professionally trained guards and force them into an open battle.
        - Mechanically, this gives soldiers a tactical upper hand on the battlefield.

## Scene 5: It's Time to Choose
- Kroman sends a messenger to Knight's Rest to inform the lord that they would like to negotiate an end to the occupation and offer friendship between their people.
    - If they reject, Kroman invites them to single combat, and if the lord kills him in single combat, his tribe will leave the forest. If the lord accepts, Kroman first tries to kill Gregory himself. If he can't, they try to launch a surprise attack. He invites him to Appleton's square. If he does not come, may the Fighter bring shame upon him.
    - Kroman applies _basic poison_ that adds 1d4 poison damage to his sword attacks.
- If they choose to burn the forest and push the Kaltiks towards their army, they will have the upper hand on the battlefield and will roll battle dice with -20 (in army battles, rolling lower on 1d100 is better).
- If they choose single combat, both sides will have no modifiers, and the main goal of the Kaltiks will be to kill Gregory.
- If they choose to march into the forest, their army will have +20 on battle dice.
- If they choose to negotiate, the two parties meet in Appleton's square diplomatically.
## Scene 6: Trial by Fire
- The oneshot ends with these possibilities:
    - A disadvantageous forest battle, where the lord decides to push into the forest to root the Kaltiks out.
    - An advantageous forest battle, where the lord decides to burn 25 km² of his lands to push the Kaltiks out of the forest and battle them.
    - A diplomatic solution, where the lord cedes Finton village to the Kaltik tribe, which ends with the Kaltiks proclaiming Zahar Pond as their new lord, resulting in the reign of House Pond splitting into two branches.
    - Single combat, where every act of treachery is expected. If Kroman understands that he cannot win against Gregory Pond, he calls in his brother to use a crossbow to finish the lord off and tries to escape into the forest afterward.
