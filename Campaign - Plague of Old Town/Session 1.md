## Scene 1: Burning Pyre
	The light that cuts through the night dizzies your eyes. The ash and soot tickle your throat. The burning pyre of the mud layer never gets smaller.
	
	The guards of Old Town mask their faces with clean white cloths, pushing carts full of the dead or those about to die. They take one after another, some sickly and barely clinging to life, begging for their lives with hoarse whispers before being thrown into the fire.
	
	Fifty meters away from the fire, there is a tent garrisoned by city guards, and in front of this tent hangs a banner. On it is a yellow burning sword, the coat of arms of House Helmanar, the rulers of Palaia province.
	
	Many people from different walks of life wait in front of this tent. From time to time, names are called, and they go in in groups of four, sometimes five. You are here because you gave your names to city officials earlier, promising that you would be one of the crawlers who can bring back magical items that might put an end to the plague, for reasons of your own.
	
	And your names are cried out by one of the acolytes who comes out of the tent, as has been done to others:
- Players goes inside to the tent, and [[Priest Ardavek]] asks who they are and what they do separately. Players introduces themselves.
- Ardavek reads out players their legal rights and obligations, and hands them out a scroll that legalizes they are employed by Lord [[Endric Helmanar]] (1 item slot).
	- Their contract allows them to seize any items, valuables, magical artifacts, and any other elements within the interests of Lord Endric Helmanar, and return them to authorities in [[Old Town]].
	- For now, they are only requrested to conduct their investigation on **Temple of Hagios Giraldi**.
	- They are given a map of the city-state of Old Town, with the temple's location marked on it (1 item slot). It is five hours of travel east from the city. Four hours along the main road to the east, followed by an hour's walk north away from the road, should lead them to the temple, carved into the side of a hill.

## Scene 3: Temple of Hagios Giraldi
	As you walk through the graslands, the level of the land starts to change visibly. And after you climb short hill, a high hill with with a large gray stone structure on its side becomes visible to you. Around this high hill, there are many other smaller hills used as burial grounds. As you get closer towards the large gray stone struture, among its crumbling columns, you see it has two large stone doors that are currently closed. You can imagine this temple is carved inside the hill.
- Inside the temple is completely dark.

**1A. Entrance**
- When the large doors are opened, they make a lot of noise with stone doors scraping against stone floors. Ask players what order they enter inside the temple.
- Just 20 feet inside, there is a pressure plate which triggers crossbow bolt trap that is shoot from the door. (Players have to make calls for their actions, such as searching for traps.)

**2A. Defenders Hall**
- Unless the party members move stealthily with DC 12 DEX check, when party members moves close to the sarcophagus inside the room, 3 **skeleton** warriors arise from their ethernal sleep, and battle with the party to prevent them going further towards Hagios Giraldi's tomb.
	- In one of the tombs, instead of a skeleton, there are 2 silver (1 item slot in total) chalices and 1 silver jug (1 item slot by itself). They worth 10 gp in total.
- There is a large marble statue at the end of the hall. In front of the marble statue, it writes "Honor those who have fallen with cleansing flames." The statue is of a female figure who wears loose robes, and only her face, her hands, her feet, and couple of strands of hair that swirls in the air you can see from these robes. DC 9 INT check is required to understand that this figure is [[Lady of Flames]].
- Behind the statue, there is a secret mechanical wall. This wall will open once the bones of the skeleton warriors inside the four tombs are given to flames.
- There is a humanoid sized hole on the left side of the hall, where gust of soft wind comes. If players carry the torch there, they require DC 12 DEX check to keep their torch out of its way, or have their torch snuffled.

**3A. Fire Bowl**
- A old craked stone bowl with ashes, soothe, and bones inside. (Human bones.)

**4A. Stair Corridor**
- There is a lever behind the mechanical door that opens and closes the door.
- This place seems to be much smaller looking at the stone floors leading to the stairs, however the environment looks to be enlarged or carved by something.
- Players can take a rest here.
