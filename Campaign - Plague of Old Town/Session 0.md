**Campaign hook:** Stop the plague of undead.

**Where does our campaign starts?**
One of the rare surviving ancient settlements in Sötonian Empire, Old Town is the capital of Palaia province and the seat of power of House Helmanar. It is known as one of the few safe havens remaining in the empire, capable of protecting those behind its walls from the evil that Prince Octavian unleashed upon the realm. Even though its connections with settlements far away have been cut, the villages surrounding the city produce enough crops to feed its population.

Campaign truths:
- A plague has taken hold of the mud layer of Old Town, spreading sickness. Those who die from the plague rise from the dead, and the local lord has ordered the sick to be kept inside the Temple of Flames while the dead are burned upon pyres of cleansing flame.
- The safest place in all of Palaia is considered to be within the walls of Old Town.
- Nearly three centuries ago, Prince Octavianus murdered his father, King Nestor, and usurped the throne of Sötonian Empire. Afterward, he unleashed devils upon the land.
- Since the *Fall*, when Prince Octavianus usurped his father, the countryside has become extremely dangerous. Supernatural creatures and devils roam the lands, and people seek protection behind fortified walls.
- People search for old artifacts from the glorious days of the empire, hoping to use them to defend themselves against the evils of the earth.
- Crawlers are sought to recover healing artifacts that might put an end to the plague of the undead. Those who succeed are promised entry to the outer layer of Old Town.
