#Legend #spoilers #Devil

He was the Supreme Archdevil (**lawful**) and one of the Somnidei of the Ninth. He was the one who opened the gates of the [[Feywild]] in _the Era of Moon_, when [[The Ninth]] brought his forces from the endlessness. He was originally one of the [[Lexicon#^Somnidei|Somnidei]] of [[Soliel]], determined to end the harm that [[Lexicon#^somniatores|Somniatores]] were creating in the universe by enforcing their selfish understanding of free will. He seats [[Throne of Skulls]] as the ruler of [[Underworld]], scheming and sending apostles to mortal world to prepare for the start of the second era of the moon, obsessed with capturing all the souls weaven for protector children and [[The Ethereal Fire|the Ethereal Fire]] shared with them to repair the paint of the universe.
## Origins of Ezekiel

**The Era of Sun**
He and Uriel were sent by Soliel herself to be trained by [[Meh'ker]] in the art of war.

Ezekiel was one of the major servants of Soliel. In _the Era of Sun_, when the Nine Somniatores shared in their council that their free-willed creations, upon receiving the offering of the Ethereal Fire from [[Lexicon#^Dormiens|Dormiens]], were creating endless creativity and free will in the lesser-willed protectors of the Dream's existence, distorting the pain of [[The Will Above All|the Will Above All]], he disagreed with leaving the maddened celestial bodies in the endlessness to their own fate.

When he was seeking answers to his inner arguments, he asked his brother [[Uriel the Golden]], whether it was not wrong to harm the fabric of the universe itself to defend the free will that the Nine saw fit. Uriel thought that the maddened and chained nature of celestial bodies in the endlessness would only bring madness and chains to the souls within the Dream. And if the lesser-willed protectors of the Dream were not free in will and creativity, then the purpose of the Dream would fail, as it would create a mass of mindless, lowly creatures without true creativity.

Ezekiel said that [[The Great Raven|the Great Raven]] did not know whether the Will Above All had awakened from its sleep. How could the Somniatores let other celestial bodies suffer just for the possibility of their wills being controlled, without knowing whether the Will Above All had awakened?

Uriel said that if, even for a possibility in the endlessness, the Dream would make a place for the free-willed in the wake of the Will Above All, then he believed that those celestial bodies who suffered would accept their fate rather than exist as slaves in the future.

Upon this, Ezekiel was drawn into silence, drowning in thoughts of celestial bodies being tortured by madness because of the acts of the Dreamers he served. He sought counsel from Soliel, asking the creator of the souls of the protectors what would be the fate of the maddened celestial bodies. After they awakened, would they be welcomed into the Dream?

Soliel said she would worry for the Dream, her companions, and her creations, for healing the wills of other celestial bodies was no one's capability but the Will Above All. Celestial bodies that were maddened would harm the Dream, and keeping them imprisoned for their own sake would be impossible with the powers of the Dreamers. Soliel said she believed in the Great Raven, and that she did not want to see a future in which, throughout all the endlessness, there would not be a will that was not enslaved to the Will Above All when it awakened.

Upon talking to Soliel, Ezekiel was horrified, for he saw a change in the philosophy of the Dream. The Dream that would let other celestial bodies exist freely caused harm to unawakened celestial bodies, as the Dreamers sharing the Ethereal Fire with their children caused harm to the fabric of the universe. The Dreamers were acting selfishly, for they were too naive to take away the creativity from their creations.

Feeling the conflict within Ezekiel's heart, the Ninth approached him and asked why he was so distraught. Ezekiel said that abandoning other celestial bodies to their madness, and Soliel putting protectors before celestials, saddened him. The Ninth said he was not alone in his thoughts, and heartened Ezekiel, asking him to open his thoughts to the other Somniatores and Somnidei in their council.

At the council, when he shared his thoughts, his words were met with heated counterarguments from many Somnidei. Uriel the Golden, his brother, gave a speech on how wrong he was, and that in the endlessness, the Dream and those who helped create it, and those who were created within it, now bear the light of free will for all of existence. His views were praised and confirmed by the other Somnidei, while the Somniatores remained silent.

When the council ended, Soliel asked Ezekiel to let go of his emotions and worries for those beyond the Dream, and, in avatar form, spend time among the protectors to empathize with and understand their existence.

Ezekiel said that he wanted to continue serving her in her celestial form, and that he did not want to step away from his true self, for he was not created, but awakened to reality with her as her satellite. Soliel asked him, until his heart was soothed, to step away from being her satellite. And he stepped down, becoming a Somnidei without a Somniator.

Later, when Uriel the Golden learned that Soliel had asked his brother to step down, he searched for his brother to ask for his forgiveness, but Ezekiel had left the Dream and wandered through the endlessness by himself.

In the endlessness, he helped the Ninth and its Somnidei soothe the maddened celestials in their sleeps. During this time, Ezekiel grew close to and witnessed how the Dreamers avoided seeing all the suffering in the endlessness that they had caused. During this time, the Ninth and Ezekiel discussed the philosophy of creation and whether the celestials had the right to open wounds in the endlessness.

When the Ninth weighed Ezekiel, it showed him the darkness that was growing in a corner of the endlessness, which it named [[Void]]. There they saw darkness touch a celestial, waking it with rage and causing it to attack the wills of Ezekiel, the Ninth, and the other Somnidei loyal to the Ninth. Using its great will and [[Élsh]], the Ninth impaled the raging celestial object, killing its will and obliterating its core.

Ezekiel shone a ray of silver light upon the darkness to draw it back, but sounds coming from the darkness told them that the end of [[Lexicon#^ThePainting|the painting]] was nigh, and there was nothing they could do to avoid it, for their thoughts, feelings, and conflicts served it.

Upon witnessing the impending doom, Ezekiel and the Ninth came to the understanding that the creation of children with free will had destroyed the endlessness from within, both spreading madness to the celestials and causing a darkness to expand throughout the endlessness.

Together, they returned to the Dream with the suffering celestials after the Ninth awakened them. Ezekiel went to the Feywild by himself before the Ninth arrived, opening the gates of the Feywild from within.

**The Era of Moon**
During the Era of Moon, he took part in sieges against the elven cities that were built around [[Alves]] in the Feywild. When he clashed with his brother Uriel, he asked him to surrender, saying that the free-willed protector children and their conflicts were destroying the endlessness.

Upon this, Soliel revealed her will and attacked Ezekiel. He managed to hold her off with the power that the Ninth had granted him, but Uriel the Golden opened a deep wound in him, wanting to put down his brother who had betrayed the Somniatores.

After being wounded by his brother, Ezekiel the Betrayer escaped into the endlessness, losing his blessing from Soliel. He healed his will and avatar, taking the form of vile reflection of what he had once been long ago.

**The Era of the Second Dream**
After the Era of Moon, the invasion of [[Void]] began. Ezekiel and the remaining followers of the Ninth made a truce with the Eight to stop the expanding darkness. Once the invasion of the Void was pushed back, it became clear that humans had turned against one another, while the races of the protector children sought dominion over one another.

Before withdrawing from the world and making the protector children open their eyes once again without knowledge of the Somniatores, the Eight and the former followers of the Ninth made a pact. The former followers of the Ninth were acknowledge as the Devils of the Underworld, taking responsibility for the souls stained with evil and the free will stained by it, in return for their aid in the battle against the Void.

Ezekiel, as the leader of the former followers of the Ninth, put his name to the pact. Now separated from the Ninth, he became the ruler of the Underworld as the Supreme Archdevil, seeking to restore the state of the Dream that existed before the protector children were created.

In **191 AA**, Ezekiel sent his apostles into the Dream to plant malice within the eastern realms of humans and gather followers in the material realm. His apostles corrupted the protector children by acting among them, and now rule the [[Sötonian Empire]]. Having seized their grasp over its rulers, they now seek to persuade and deceive the protector children who hide behind the flickering light of the Somniatores.
