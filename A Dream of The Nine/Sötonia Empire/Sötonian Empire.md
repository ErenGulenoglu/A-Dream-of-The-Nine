Sötonian Empire is a culmination of large city-states brought together under the rule of Nestor I Dikarios. The total area of the empire is approximately 4 million square kilometers.

Once ruling over a large part of the Earthfather, it is now drowning in a bloodbath of unending civil wars and horrors that have been unleashed upon the realm after Prince Octavian murdered his father, Nestor I. Nestor is now believed to have risen to godhood as King Nestor among many hero cult worshippers.

It is now known as the Old Empire, ruled by Prince Octavianus. Its capital is [Nestorpolis](Nestorpolis).

**Fading of Ancestries**
The powers of races in this part of the Dream are faded compared to those who live in _Realm of the Awakened_, for the light of [Alves](Alves) does not reach these lands. Those who are born in these lands carry less of their ancestral inheritance compared to their ancestors and those who live in _Realm of the Awakened_.
