Sötonians practice hero cults, and believe in nature spirits. They prey and honor to dead mortals who acheived greatness in their life, and believed to ascend into godhood, and elements of the land that they believe to brought their bodies out of mud, water, fire, and air. 
## Deities in Sötonia

**[[King Nestor]]** - _Lawful_
A legendary king who brought most of Sötonia's rule in his lifetime who is the patron of most valiant warriors. He ascended to godhood long ago and is the embodiment of leadership and justice.

**[[Daphnyr]]** - _Neutral_
The earthfather who created humans from natures womb herself. After the folly of humans who took Prince Octavianus as their ruler, he is believed to be angry and brings the dead back to life to take the life of those who he gave once.

**[[Lady of Flames]]** - _Neutral_
Lady of Flames placed the the spark of soul inside humans and broke them free from Daphnyr. All fires, light, and shadows are hers to control.

**[[Leda]]** - _Chaotic Good_
The lady moonlight. She shows paths for those seeking knowledge, backed into a corner, and tyrannized. She is the goddess of help, knowledge, magic, and secrets.

**[[Archdevil Octavianus|Prince Octavianus]]** - _Chaotic Evil_
The tyrant of Sötonia who is feared by all. He usurped his father King Nestor, let evil loose upon the lands, and bathes in the blood of his subjects. He is ruler of devils and undead, enjoying corrupt tastes from his throne in the capital [[Nestorpolis]].
