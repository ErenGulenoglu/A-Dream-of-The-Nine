One of the rare surviving ancient settlements in [[Sötonian Empire]], Old Town is the capital of [[Palaia]] province and the seat of power of [[House Helmanar]]. It is known as one of the few safe havens remaining in the empire, capable of protecting those behind its walls from the evil that Prince Octavian unleashed upon the realm. Even though its connections with settlements far away have been cut, the villages surrounding the city produce enough crops to feed its population.

Old Town survives through an uneasy alliance between mages, clergy, and nobility.
- Exports: Crawlers, Tools, Arms, Horses, Jewelry, any goods or services available
- Imports: Crops, Livestock, Magical Artifacts, Raw Materials
- Government: Lordship
- Races: Humans, Elves, Dwarves, Half-Orcs, Halflings, Goblins
- Religion: Lady of Flames
- Population: 100,000

## Defenses
In addition to its garrison and town guard, Lord Helmanar's professional army forms the inner defense of the city. There is an extra layer of invisible arcane _Wall of Force_ that encloses the villages and farms around the city. To maintain this wall, 100 humanoids are sacrificed by the mages every day. The Temple of the Lady of Flames preaches for people to surrender themselves to the Lady, both to maintain the magical wall and to preserve the hopes of the people. They replenish the number of sacrifices with the people migrating to the city more with every passing day.

Old Town has two circles. The inner circle is where the nobility, guildsmen, soldiers, influential clergy, and Lord Helmanar live. This circle is smaller and guarded by an additional layer of town guards who watch those entering and leaving. In the outer circle live those who do not have enough wealth to afford a comfortable life in times of doom. Those who cannot afford the luxury lifestyle but still a citizcen of the city live within this outer layer.

The city has many walls, and from a bird's-eye view, it resembles a large circle. However, with the coming waves of migrants every day, another layer has been built outside the walls, where every kind of dirty business can be seen. It is called the mud circle.
