**Knight's Rest** is the ancestral seat of [[House Pond]], located within [[Ample Grove]] in western [[Patria]]. It was built on the western shore of [[Blue Pond]], with construction beginning in **585 AA**.

It is a large fortified manor with blue roof tiles and gray stone walls, enclosed by high outer walls that extend toward the forest. Its lakeside foundations are built directly into Blue Pond. The doors facing the lake are thick, iron-bound, and defensible against sieges, forcing any attacker to swim to reach them. The manor's walls have more arrow slits than windows, and guards are stationed from the highest level of the manor down to the outer walls. The militaristic nature of House Pond shaped Knight's Rest into more of a castle than a residence, a fortress built in what seemed like a paradise beyond the reach of enemies, yet always prepared for the day they might come.

The manor has three above-ground stories containing numerous rooms, along with several underground levels designed to house a much larger garrison in times of war. During peacetime, it is guarded by sixty men. Its defenses operate at maximum efficiency with a garrison of one hundred soldiers, though it is capable of housing even more when necessary.
