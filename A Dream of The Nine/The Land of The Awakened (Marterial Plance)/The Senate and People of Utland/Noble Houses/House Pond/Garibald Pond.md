**Garibald Pond** was born in **799 AA** as the eldest son of [[Sir Maryn Pond]]. In **836 AA**, he rode off to war with his father and his two brothers, **Gelther Pond** and **Gerwald Pond**. He witnessed the burning of [[Alves|the Alf Tree of Veretia]]. After flanking the elven coalition's wizards with a cavalry charge to eliminate critical enemy forces, his unit was ambushed by a bronze dragon that descended from between the clouds onto the battlefield, burning the cavalry of [[Galvyn II von Ewfai]]. Wounded and lying on the battlefield, he was forced to mercy-kill his brother **Gelther Pond**. Garibald survived the [[Battle of Embers]] and was later healed by elves.

After _the War of Flames_, he adapted to a life as a nomad rather than a noble. In the late summer of **857 AA**, he returned to [[Ample Grove]] under the name Cato, the name of his old household guard captain, after learning that his youngest brother, [[Sir Gregory Pond]], had become the new lord of [[House Pond]]. He returned to meet the only surviving member of his family and observe him from afar, wanting to see what kind of man his younger brother had become. He disguised himself as Cato, the family's old captain of the guard, who had gone to _the War of Flames_ with their host.

After arriving at Knight's Rest, he found that his brother was away. A group of bandits had attacked the guardsmen of House Pond and forced the villagers to provide recruits and surrender their goods. Seeing his family's soldiers hiding behind their ancestral walls while leaving the villagers to their fate against the bandits, he argued with the captain of the household guard, Titus, that they should act immediately before the bandits turned the people's support against House Pond. Seeing bandits operating so deep within the heart of the province of [[Partia]], Garibald understood that someone must have had a hand in these treacherous attacks against House Pond.
