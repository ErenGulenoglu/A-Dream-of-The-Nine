**House Pond** is a noble house, a landed hereditary knighthood, located in [[Patria]], the northern province of [[The Senate and People of Utland|the republic]]. Their ties trace back to the cavalry of [[Ewyn I von Ewfai]]. Their seat of power, [[Knight's Rest]], lies within [[Ample Grove]], built on the eastern side of [[Blue Pond]]. House Pond is favored by their subjects, as they maintain light tax policies in favor of their villagers. House Pond rules over the villages of [[Finton]], and [[Appleton]].

Historically a militaristic family renowned for raising knights, they lost all male heirs but the youngest of the senior branch (Blue Ponds) after _the War of the Flames_. Their coat of arms is a green fish on a blue field, and their motto is “Blood runs thicker than water.”

## History
<hr style="margin: 0;">

***Sir Maryn Pond (783 AA - 857 AA)*** 
^MarynPond

Sir Maryn was born in 783 AA. He was the eldest son of **Sir Samuel Pond**. He succeeded his father at the age of 14, upon Sir Samuel’s early death. In 784 AA, he married his betrothed, **[[Idil Pond]]**. He was known for his martial prowess. He unhorsed [[Louise Rose|Sir Louise Rose]], a renowned knight and brother of [[House Rose#^JacquesI|Duke Jacques I Rose]] in 808 AA in the [[Tournament of Roses]].

Sir Maryn had his firstborn son, **Garibald Pond**, in 799 AA. His second-born son, **Gelther Pond**, was born in 802 AA. His third-born son, **Gerwald Pond**, was born in 806 AA. In 834 AA, his youngest son, [[Sir Gregory Pond]], was born.

In 836 AA, when [[Galvyn II von Ewfai|King Galvyn II von Ewfai]] called his banners to march against [[Veretia]], Sir Maryn gathered his 100 household soldiers and levies to join the king’s campaign. He and his sons witnessed the burning of [[Alves|the Alf Tree of Veretia]]. At the [[Battle of Embers]], while leading a cavalry flank, he lost his sons **Garibald**, **Gelther**, and **Gerwald** to dragon fire. In 840 AA, his wife, **Lady Idil Nevenir**, passed away.

In 851 AA, Sir Maryn’s younger brother, **Marvin Pond**, passed away at the age of 65. Through Marvin’s line, a cadet branch of the family, known as _Green Pond_ of House Pond, was formed. The elder son of **Marvin Pond**, [[Zahar Pond]], claimed that the house’s fate could not be left in the hands of a boy who did not know what it meant to be a lord, nor in the hands of an old man who was weakening the house by failing to enforce proper taxation. In response, Sir Maryn ousted his nephews and nieces from Knight’s Rest.

In 857 AA, on his deathbed, Sir Maryn knighted his youngest son, Gregory, in front of witnesses.

***Sir Gregory Pond (857 AA Summer - Current)*** 

Sir Gregory Pond was born in 834 AA as the youngest son of Sir Maryn Pond. He succeeded his father at the age of 23.

Sir Gregory Pond took his knighthood vows next to his father's deathbed. Before Sir Maryn Pond passed away, he knighted his son. Priest Cerdo, Captain of household guard Titus, and [[Sir Alexander Brokentower, the Shieldbreaker]] witnessed him getting knighted.

After Sir Maryn's death, Sir Gregory Pond's inheritance was challenged by his eldest cousin Zahar Pond, and was opposed by cadet branch of House Pond, the _Greenponds_. He was summoned to [[Wolfkeep]] by Baron [[Diethard Farkvel]] to defend his claim. After hearing the defense of both claimants, Baron Diethard Farkel declared that Zahar Pond was the true inheritor of House Pond as the eldest male in House Pond, since the knighthood oath he took was binding him to kings who no longer exists within the government body of SPQU, his knighthood oath, and therefore his claim to the lordship of House Pond as landed knight, was invalid.

Upon Baron's decision that unfavored him, using the right that came from his blood, Sir Gregory Pond demanded trial by combat to defend his claim. A day before his trial, he fought as champion of [[Iris Lavoine]], defending her and her son, [[Stamatis Lavoine]]'s inheritance to [[House Lavoine]]. He fought againts [[Sir Robyn Grape]], the sour grape, and won by killing Sir Robyn Grape through smashing his head with shield, which earned Sir Gregory Pond the name, redshield.

On his trial day, Sir Alexander Brokentower, the Shieldbreaker fought in his stead as his champion against [[Gentoris Wooddreamer]]. Sir Alexander Brokentower won the trial, and spared the elven opponents life.
