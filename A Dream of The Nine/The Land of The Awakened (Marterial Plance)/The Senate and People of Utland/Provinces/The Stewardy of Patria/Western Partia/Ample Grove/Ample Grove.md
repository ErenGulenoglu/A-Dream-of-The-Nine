**Ample Grove** is a small forest best known as a small forest that signifies friendship and peace between Kingdom of Utland and [[Ard Caëd]] through its [[Moon Tree|moon tree]]s which seeds were donated by  the Council of Elders, was a small forest located in eastern borders of western [[Partia]].
## Geography
<hr style="margin: 0;">

The [[Blue River]] flows down from [[Mount Tarnesah]] into [[Blue Pond]], spreading into creeks further up north.

**Geographical Features**
Mount Tarnesah settling on the south-west edge, sends Blue River in a way that draws the border between north and south parts of the forest, leaving east as bare forest without any natural separation landscapes. Main road that descends south from [[Ewyngard]] follows skirts of Mount Tarnesah gave way to south. The whole grove is approximately 100 kilometer square.

**Overland Travel**
- From northern entrance of the grove to southern entrance takes approximately **8-10 hours** at a normal pace.

**Flora & Fauna**
Apart from High Forest, moon trees are only found in Ample Grove. They are accepted as magical by local folk as they change the land around them to be more fruitful and fecund. Moon trees grove blue flowers, and gave a fruit called [[Ai'nish|ai'nsh]] that looked like blue apples that had small grains like of fig inside. From ai'nsh, a blue drink called moon wine is made. Felling moon trees for wood was forbidden, but when fallen they gave soft wood with blue tinge. Moon trees are denser on south side of the forest than of north.

Apart from moon trees, there were oaks, elms, pines, spruce, birch, and chestnut trees. All kinds of fruitful trees within the forest was harvested and tended. Within the forest  deer, foxes, coyotes, beavers, racoons, squirrels were usually; bears were seldom seen.
## History
<hr style="margin: 0;">

The Council of Elders of High Forest offered [[Owyn I von Ewfai]] to plant valuable moon tree seeds of elves within his land as a sign of friendship between their countries in 585 AA. As the trees were planted by elves to grove around Blue Pond, the area was named Ample Grove. To settle on this natural beauty, Owyn I von Ewfai decreed that the grove shall we parted between his well respected two magister equitum (master of the horse), Magister Fluvius who later found [[House Pond]], and Magister Publius who later found [[House Moon]].
