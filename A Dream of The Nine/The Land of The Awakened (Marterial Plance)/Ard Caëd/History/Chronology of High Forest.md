## What is Sunrise
- Contrary to the historians of [[Utland]], the [[Ard Caëd]] elves use the dawn that first shone upon their forests to label and keep records of history. High Elves claim that historians of Utland point out that the gods left the earth as the sun rose.
- Elves lived long before there was a Sun in _Tawnaur_ (Tree Earth). As they lived close to their [[Alves|Alf Tree]] in the High Forest, they did not wander into complete darkness to become lost, or have their lives shortened. They claim that they lived for tens of thousands of years before the sun rose, and that they witnessed no "Eight" wandering the realm.
- The chronologies of the High Forest and [[Veretia]] agree that there was an era when there was no sun, as the halflings and gnomes of Veretia lived beneath the Alf Tree of Veretia. However, conflicts still arise in historical accounts, as halflings and gnomes are not immortal like the elves.
