#Village #Humans #Utland 
**Appleton** was the first settlement of [[House Pond]]. After King [[Owyn I von Ewfai]] decreed that northern [[Ample Grove]] was property of **Magister Fluvius**, the [[Sötonian]] migrants built their homes inside the grove, towards south of [[Blue Pond]].

Appleton survives through forest farming, wood cutting, winemaking, and hunting.
-  Exports: Apple, [[Ai'nish]], Wine, Wood.
- Government: Mayor
- Races: Humans, Halflings
- Region: [[Patria]]
- Religion: [[The Lightkeeper]]
- Population: 800
## Government
<hr style="margin: 0;">

The governing responsibility is given to the local Church of the Lightkeeper. The church keeps official records and hands them to [[Knight's Rest]]. 

## Defenses
<hr style="margin: 0;">

Twenty-five militia guard this village at all times, a number that is more than needed. They act as officials that villagers can go to make their needs known to Lord Pond. Their numbers are due to House Pond wanting to keep their numbers up compared to their neighbors at all times.
